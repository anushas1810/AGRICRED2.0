# Phase 5 — Solution-first engine
The execute endpoint creates six suggested actions:
1. Government subsidy matching
2. Crop insurance enrollment guidance
3. Crop-plan diversification
4. Loan restructuring review
5. Market linkage / mandi comparison
6. Early-warning and protection alerts
- Actions are recommendations, not automatic government enrollment or bank-side loan changes.
- Verify: POST `/api/solutions/{loanId}/execute`.
